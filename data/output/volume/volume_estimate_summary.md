# Lunar Subsurface Ice Volume Estimate

## Summary
- **Total Ice Volume**: 3.38e+04 m³
- **Uncertainty Range**: 2.36e+04 – 4.39e+04 m³
- **Total Area**: 9.32e+03 m²
- **Mean Ice Fraction**: 0.724
- **Max Ice Fraction**: 0.745
- **Depth Assumed**: 5.0 m
- **Valid Pixels**: 373

## Methodology
- **Mixing Model**: maxwell-garnett
- **Regolith Dielectric**: 3.0 (range: 2.5–3.5)
- **Ice Dielectric**: 3.15 (range: 3.1–3.2)
- **Uncertainty**: Propagated via Monte Carlo sampling over dielectric constant ranges

## Interpretation
This estimate represents the ice volume within the top 5.0 m of regolith
in the doubly-shadowed candidate regions where repeat-pass validated ice-likelihood
criteria are met with high or medium confidence.

The uncertainty range reflects genuine physical uncertainty in the dielectric
properties of lunar polar regolith and ice at cryogenic temperatures, not
measurement error.
